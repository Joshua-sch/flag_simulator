FLAG FEASIBILITY SIMULATOR — MODEL ASSUMPTIONS
LinChris Capital
=====================================================

This document summarizes every assumption built into the Flag Feasibility
Simulator: where the underlying actuals came from, how the model turns
those actuals into projected outcomes, and the specific default numbers
currently in use for Hotel 1620 and Inn at Middletown.


1. DATA SOURCES (per property)
-------------------------------

Hotel 1620 (Plymouth Harbor, Plymouth MA — 177 rooms)
  - Occupancy, ADR, and comp-set benchmarks: STR "Tab 4 — Competitive Set
    Report," Running 12 Month column (trailing to May 2026). This is the
    most representative full-year figure available, rather than a single
    month's snapshot.
  - Group vs. transient occupancy split: derived from the BOB (Business on
    the Books) report, Running 12 Month (Jun 2025–May 2026), using the
    actual room-night mix (group rooms sold vs. individual rooms sold as a
    share of total rooms sold). That proportion is then applied to the
    STR-sourced total occupancy figure, since STR and BOB's own total
    occupancy numbers didn't match exactly (STR is treated as the more
    reliable total).

Inn at Middletown (Middletown, CT — 100 rooms)
  - Occupancy, ADR, and comp-set benchmarks: same STR "Comp" tab, Running
    12 Month methodology as Hotel 1620.
  - Group vs. transient occupancy split: derived from the ROB Master
    Workbook's revenue split (group vs. transient revenue), averaged across
    its two available trailing-year columns, converted to a room-night
    proportion and applied to the STR total occupancy figure the same way
    as 1620.

Bottom line: for both properties, STR determines "how big is the pie"
(total occupancy, ADR, comp set), and the property-specific revenue system
(BOB for 1620, ROB for Middletown) determines "how the pie splits" between
group and transient business.

3. HOW THE MODEL PROJECTS EACH FLAG SCENARIO
-----------------------------------------------

For each flag type (Hard: Marriott/Hilton/IHG-tier; Soft: Autograph/
Tapestry/Vignette-tier), the model takes five base-case assumptions you set
directly:
  - Franchise + royalty fee (% of gross revenue)
  - Expected transient occupancy lift (percentage points)
  - Expected group occupancy lift (percentage points)
  - ADR impact (%)
  - PIP (property improvement plan) cost per room

From that single base case, "Optimistic" and "Underperforming" variants are
generated automatically (not independently estimated):

  * Transient/group lift swing ±50% of their own value (so hard flag's
    +12pp base becomes +18pp optimistic / +6pp underperforming)
  * Fee multiplies ×0.85 (optimistic) / ×1.15 (pessimistic)
  * PIP cost multiplies ×0.70 (optimistic) / ×1.30 (pessimistic)
  * ADR impact swings by a flat ±2.5 percentage points

Then revenue = (base transient occ + lift) × room-nights × ADR, same for
group, minus fee% of gross, minus PIP:

  - Room-nights available = rooms × 365
  - Transient revenue = (current transient occ% + transient lift) ×
    room-nights × scenario ADR
  - Group revenue = (current group occ% + group lift) × room-nights ×
    scenario ADR
  - Gross revenue = transient + group revenue
  - Fee cost = gross revenue × franchise fee %
  - PIP cost = (PIP $/room × rooms) amortized straight-line over the
    chosen number of years (7 years by default)
  - Net revenue = gross revenue − fee cost − amortized PIP cost
  - This is compared against "Stay Independent" (current actuals, zero fee,
    zero PIP) to get net revenue vs. baseline, and a payback period in years
    is calculated from the PIP cost vs. annual incremental net revenue.

Current default flag assumptions (base case, both properties start here —
fully editable per property):

  Hard flag:  11.0% fee | +12.0pp transient lift | +0.0pp group lift |
              −3.0% ADR impact | $18,000/room PIP
  Soft brand:  7.0% fee | +6.0pp transient lift  | +0.0pp group lift |
              +1.0% ADR impact | $7,500/room PIP
  PIP amortization period: 7 years (default, adjustable 1–20 years)


4. KEY STRUCTURAL ASSUMPTIONS / LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------------------------

  - Group occupancy lift defaults to 0pp for both flag types — the model
    assumes group/meetings business is relationship- and market-driven, not
    brand-driven, and is held flat unless you explicitly set a group lift.
  - Transient and group segments are assumed to earn the SAME ADR in the
    revenue math — there's no separate transient-rate vs. group-rate
    assumption. (This means how you split an occupancy gain between
    transient/group doesn't change total revenue, only which line item it
    shows up under.)
  - There is currently no linkage between PIP dollars spent and how much
    occupancy or ADR actually improves — the occupancy lift and PIP cost
    are set independently by you, not tied to each other by a formula.
  - The occupancy-lift assumptions (the +12pp/+6pp defaults above) are
    manually set, not derived from the comp-set occupancy gap. We discussed
    and tested (outside the live app, not yet built in) an alternative
    approach that ties expected occupancy lift to a defined percentage of
    the actual gap to comp set — that version is not implemented in the
    tool as of this writing.
  - Comp-set figures (occupancy/ADR) are currently informational/display
    only in the app — they are not yet wired into the revenue projections.


5. WHAT'S EDITABLE VS. FIXED
--------------------------------
  Editable per property, live in the app: room count, current occupancy/
  ADR/comp set, transient/group split, all five base-case flag assumptions
  per flag type, PIP amortization period.

  Fixed by the model's internal formulas (not user-facing sliders): the
  ±50% optimistic/underperforming swing rule, the fee/PIP multiplier
  ranges, and the ADR swing of ±2.5pp described in Section 3.
